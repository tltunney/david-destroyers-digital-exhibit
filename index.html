Mouse & keyboard

  • WASD walk
  • Mouse look around
  • Shift walk faster  Space jump
  • E or Click view an exhibit
  • 1–9 jump to a room
  • M map  N music
  • Esc pause

Phone & tablet

  • Left thumb stick: walk
  • Drag anywhere else: look around
  • Jump button: jump
  • Tap an exhibit: view it
  • Turn your phone sideways for the best view
